## DOCUMENT 4: Data Protection, Privacy & IT Act Undertaking

```text
====================================================================================================

====================================================================================================

To:
The Principal / Head of Institution
[Name of College / University]

Subject: Undertaking regarding Data Privacy, Security, and Compliance of Hosteleaze Software.

Dear Sir/Madam,

We, HOSTELEAZE, hereby formally declare and provide this undertaking regarding the data privacy, 
security infrastructure, and statutory compliance of the Hosteleaze software deployed at your institution:

1. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE:
   Hosteleaze is designed and maintained in adherence to:
   - The Information Technology Act, 2000 (and Information Technology Amendment Act, 2008).
   - The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India).
   - CERT-In cybersecurity guidelines for educational web applications.

2. BIOMETRIC & FACE DATA ENCRYPTION:
   - No raw facial photographs are stored as plain files for authentication. Face embeddings/descriptors 
     are mathematically vectorized and stored using AES-256 / SHA-256 standard encryption.
   - Student location data is checked transiently for geofence validation and is not tracked outside 
     stipulated attendance windows.

3. NETWORK & DATABASE SECURITY:
   - End-to-End SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption is enforced across all communication channels.
   - Access control is governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with secure session handling.
   - Daily automated database snapshots and encrypted cloud backups are maintained.

4. NON-DISCLOSURE & DATA INTEGRITY:
   Hosteleaze does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose any student or institutional data to third 
   parties or advertising networks.
```
